Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 25, 2026

Pixel Tone is a camera-based application that does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

Camera Access

The application uses your device's camera solely to provide real-time color extraction from the camera preview. No photos or videos are stored, saved, uploaded, or shared by the app. All processing happens instantly on your device and no visual data persists after the color is analyzed.

Advertising

This app displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use the Advertising ID to serve personalised or non-personalised ads based on your device settings. You can reset or opt out of personalised ads anytime from your device's Google settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

You can learn more about AdMob's policies here.

Age Requirement

This app is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

Data Security

No data is stored by Pixel Tone. All processing occurs exclusively on your device, and no data is transmitted to any external server. The camera permission is used only for live color detection and is not used for any other purpose.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
